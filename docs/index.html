Everything you need

Simple on the surface.
Serious underneath.

Built on libtorrent, the same battle-tested engine behind qBittorrent and Deluge, with a native Mac app on top.

Locks to your VPN

Bind Peerbloom to any active VPN. It follows the tunnel through reconnects, and nothing ever slips out over your normal connection.

Privacy mode

One switch: VPN or SOCKS5 proxy only, encryption required, anonymous mode on, and no local broadcasts.

Malware warnings

Spots apps, installers, scripts and fakes like movie.mp4.exe before you download, and quarantines everything it saves.

Watch while it downloads

Stream mode fetches pieces in order, so open videos start playing right away.

Feels like a Mac app

Menu bar speeds, notifications, colour tags, a slow-mode schedule, and it pauses before your disk fills up.

Make it yours

Six built-in themes, and a Theme Maker that designs a new one from a few questions.

Privacy, honestly

If the VPN drops,
nothing leaks.

BitTorrent is public by design: peers can see the address you connect from. Peerbloom makes your own VPN watertight. Bind it once, and when the tunnel goes down, traffic stops instead of falling back to your home connection.

  • No ads, no accounts, no analytics. Not now, not later.
  • Shows the address peers see, so you know your VPN works.
  • Detects your VPN app and opens it in one click.
  • SOCKS5 and HTTP proxies, with the password kept in your Keychain.

Safety, built in

Movies don't need
to be programs.

The biggest danger in torrents isn't the protocol. It's malware dressed up as something harmless. Peerbloom checks every file list and flags apps, installers, scripts and disguised files, with one click to skip them. Everything it saves gets the same quarantine mark as a Safari download, so macOS checks it before it can run.

Questions

Good to know

Is Peerbloom really free?

Yes. It's open source under the GNU GPL v3, with no ads, no paid tier and no accounts. Anyone can read the code, and changed versions must stay open too, so it can't be turned into a closed app with ads later.

Why does macOS say it can't verify Peerbloom?

Peerbloom isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID. To open it the first time: open Peerbloom once and close the warning, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once. Or build it yourself from the source on GitHub.

Does Peerbloom hide my IP address?

No torrent app can on its own: BitTorrent shares your address with the peers you trade with. What Peerbloom does is make your own VPN or SOCKS5 proxy watertight, show you the address peers see, and stop all traffic if the VPN drops. Peerbloom doesn't include a VPN.

Is BitTorrent legal?

BitTorrent is a legal technology, used to share Linux releases, open movies, game updates and large public datasets. Downloading or sharing copyrighted material without permission is not, so please respect the law where you live. Peerbloom has no built-in torrent search.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon (M1 and newer).

Can I make my own theme?

Yes. In Settings › Appearance, click Copy Theme Maker and paste it into any AI assistant. It asks a few questions and writes a .peerbloomtheme file. Double-click it to install. You can also write one by hand; the format is on GitHub.
